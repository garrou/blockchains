Explorer

Watch blocks fill up, in 3D

Every transaction is a tower. Its footprint is the room it takes in its block; its height and color are what it paid (or, where nobody bids, what it is). Pick a blockchain: the same engine draws all of them.

Bitcoin

A block about every 10 minutes. Tower: size in virtual bytes, fee rate in sat/vB.

  • The next block, projected from the mempool
  • Search a height, a hash, a transaction or an address
  • Follow an address in 3D
Open Bitcoin →

Ethereum

A block every 12 seconds. Tower: gas used, gas price in gwei.

  • Base fee of the next block, ETH burned, finality
  • Transactions, token movements, ENS names
  • A guess of the next block from pending transactions
Open Ethereum →

Solana

A block every 0.4 second, one in ten shown. Tower: compute (estimated), priority fee paid.

  • Validators' votes, failed transactions, priority bidders
  • Instructions, balance changes, program log
  • Accounts with their latest transactions
Open Solana →

XRP Ledger

A ledger every 3 to 5 seconds, final once validated. Tower: ledger objects touched, colored by kind of transaction.

  • Payments, exchange orders, trust lines, AMM pools, NFTs
  • Failed transactions, fee now, ledger mix
  • Accounts with their latest transactions
Open XRP →

The data comes straight from public nodes (mempool.space, publicnode, xrplcluster.com): no account, no key. Those services see your IP address and what you look up. The code is static: open these pages from a folder or any web host.
